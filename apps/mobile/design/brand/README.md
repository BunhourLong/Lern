# Lern logo

The neon-green, square-cut L is the primary mark for rides and express delivery. Use it on the launcher and driver helmets. The lowercase **lern** wordmark spells out the name where it helps with recognition.

On sign-in, center the L in the upper area and reserve roughly the lower third for sign-in actions. On the home screen, place a smaller L in the upper-left corner. Preserve the transparent square cut at both sizes.

[Standalone vector](lern-mark.svg) · [Approved mobile concept](mobile-concept.png)

The mockup illustrates logo placement. Google sign-in is an example in the artwork. The planned sign-in flow uses phone OTP.

Decided 2026-11-19 (UI.6c review):
- Light theme: the L is ink (`brand` token, `#191919`); green on a light background is 1.26:1. Dark theme: green `#89F597`.
- The app's `primary` follows the logo: `#1B7339` light, `#89F597` dark.
- Launcher: the L is 56% of the iOS icon's height and 44% of the Android adaptive canvas (its diagonal fits the 66 dp safe circle). `export-icons.sh` writes both.
- Khmer companion wordmark លឿន: Kantumruy Pro Bold (already bundled).
